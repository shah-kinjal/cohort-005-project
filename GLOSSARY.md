# Cadence Glossary

The shared language for the course marketplace. Use these terms in code, UI copy, emails and docs.

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a Purchase, and its outcome. Each Refund moves from Pending to Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, Cancel my purchase, money back

**Pending refund**:
A Refund that has been requested and has not yet received its final decision. It is either waiting for a first approval or, for a large Refund, for its Second approval.
_Avoid_: Open refund, Requested refund, Pending chargeback

**Approved refund**:
A Refund that has received its final approval; the learner is owed the full amount they paid and loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Completed refund, Processed refund

**Declined refund**:
A Refund that a Support agent or Admin has rejected; nothing is sent back, and the decision is final at any amount.
_Avoid_: Rejected refund, Denied refund

**Support agent**:
A person on our team whose job is to carry out Refund reviews. Admins can do the same.
_Avoid_: CS, CS team, Customer service, Approver

**Refund review**:
A Support agent's or Admin's decision to approve or decline a Pending refund. Nobody may review their own Refund.
_Avoid_: Refund processing, Refund handling

**Approval threshold**:
The amount paid ($25.00) at or above which a Refund needs two approvals instead of one.
_Avoid_: Refund limit, Second-approval limit

**First approval**:
The approval a Refund at or above the Approval threshold receives from its first reviewer. It does not yet make the Refund an Approved refund.
_Avoid_: Preliminary approval, Pre-approval

**Second approval**:
The approval, by a different reviewer, that turns a Refund at or above the Approval threshold into an Approved refund.
_Avoid_: Final approval, Countersign

**Awaiting second approval**:
The state of a Refund that has its First approval and is waiting for its Second approval. It is still a Pending refund.
_Avoid_: Half-approved, Partially approved

**Refund window**:
The 14 calendar days from the day of Purchase during which a learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, Cancellation window

**Chargeback** is reserved for its payments meaning, a dispute the learner's card issuer raises against us. We do not model chargebacks, and the word must not be used for a Refund.
