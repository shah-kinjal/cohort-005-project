# Cadence Glossary

The shared language for the course marketplace. Use these terms in code, UI copy, emails and docs.

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a Purchase.
_Avoid_: Chargeback (a card-issuer dispute we do not model), Reversal, Cancellation

**Pending refund**:
A Refund with no final decision yet.
_Avoid_: Open refund, Requested refund

**Approved refund**:
A Refund with its final approval; the learner loses course access.

**Declined refund**:
A Refund rejected for good; nothing is sent back.

**Support agent**:
A person who carries out Refund reviews; Admins can too.
_Avoid_: CS team, Approver

**Refund review**:
Approving or declining a Pending refund; nobody reviews their own.

**Approval threshold**:
The amount paid at or above which a Refund needs two approvals.

**First approval**:
The first reviewer's approval of a Refund at or above the Approval threshold.

**Second approval**:
A different reviewer's approval that makes it an Approved refund.

**Awaiting second approval**:
A Refund with its First approval; still a Pending refund.

**Refund window**:
The period after Purchase in which a learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window
