# Cadence Glossary

The shared language for the course marketplace. Use these terms in code, UI copy, emails and docs.

## Refunds

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a Purchase, and its outcome. Each Refund moves from Pending to Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, Cancel my purchase, money back

**Pending refund**:
A Refund that has been requested and not yet resolved by a person on our team.
_Avoid_: Open refund, Requested refund, Pending chargeback

**Approved refund**:
A Refund that our team has accepted; the learner is sent back the amount refunded.
_Avoid_: Completed refund, Processed refund

**Declined refund**:
A Refund that our team has rejected; nothing is sent back.
_Avoid_: Rejected refund, Denied refund

**Refund window**:
The 14 calendar days from the day of Purchase during which a learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, Cancellation window

**Chargeback** is reserved for its payments meaning, a dispute the learner's card issuer raises against us. We do not model chargebacks, and the word must not be used for a Refund.
